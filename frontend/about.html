FLAREWATCH
Zero-Dependency High-Throughput SIEM & Threat Detection Engine

FlareWatch is a purpose-built, high-performance SIEM system designed for raw network log ingestion. Utilizing a hand-rolled Aho-Corasick engine in Rust, it detects threats in real-time, indexes logs instantly for deep search, and streams telemetry directly to this glassmorphic dashboard—without relying on heavy external dependencies.
